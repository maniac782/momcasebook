Mansions of Madness Ledger

Privacy

Mansions of Madness Ledger is a free, personal hobby site for logging board game plays. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

What is stored

  • Your sign-in (Google or email and password), handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your name and email address, when you joined and last visited, and how many plays you've logged.
  • What you log: scenarios, dates, results, the names you type for players and investigators, rules and notes. Only you can see your plays.
  • Feedback you send, and technical details of errors the site hits while you're signed in (message, page, browser), so they can be fixed.

Everything is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The site's admin can see the account list (names, emails, play counts), feedback and errors, but not your plays. A copy of your plays is also kept in your browser so the app works offline.

Your choices

Settings has Download CSV and Download backup for a copy of your plays, Delete all plays, and Delete my account, which removes your plays, your scenarios, your profile and your sign-in.

Other people's names

If you write down who you played with, use names they're happy with; they're only visible to you.
